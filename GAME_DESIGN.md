# JIBON RUNNER — Game Design

## Vision
Create an endless 3D running game that feels like running through Bangladesh. The game should be welcoming to a broad age range, easy to control and strongly supported by high-quality environmental audio.

## Bangladesh world
Build realistic, recognizably Bangladeshi locations:
- Rural paved and earthen roads, paddy fields, ponds, bamboo groves, village homes and roadside tea stalls.
- Village markets, local shops, bus stands and rural highways.
- Town and city streets with Bangla shop signs, road signs, buses, trucks, cars, rickshaws, CNGs and motorcycles.
- Bridges, flyovers, rail crossings and station surroundings where appropriate.
- Day, evening, night, rain and mist variants.

Use correctly spelled Bangla signage and locally appropriate architecture, road markings, vehicles and props. Avoid generic foreign city assets where possible.

## Player
A realistic human 3D character with run, lane-change, jump, slide, stumble/fall, idle/rest and eating animations. Character selection and clothing can be added after the core loop works.

## Controls
- Swipe left/right: change lane.
- Swipe up: jump.
- Swipe down: slide.
- Optional large touch buttons for accessibility.
- Pause button always available during a run.

## Endless gameplay
Three-lane runner. Spawn road segments and obstacles ahead; recycle or remove segments behind the player to control memory. Increase challenge gradually while keeping obstacle patterns fair and readable. There is no final level or fixed ending.

Obstacles may include buses, trucks, cars, rickshaws, motorcycles, road barriers, potholes, construction objects and safe-to-dodge rail-crossing hazards. Do not create graphic injury scenes.

## Collectibles and progression
Coins, missions, daily rewards, distance records, character outfits and unlockable cosmetic items. No gambling or betting.

## Food Break System
- At 5,000 m: breakfast break.
- At 10,000 m: find a food stall / food choice break.
- At 15,000 m: lunch break.
- At 20,000 m: water and rest break.
- Continue with additional configurable milestones.

Pause the running simulation during a break. Show a Bangladeshi roadside food location, food choices, optional in-game coin purchases, eating/rest animation and a clear Resume button. Some food should be free. Breaks must never cause loss of a valid run.

## Audio — a primary feature
Create a layered, high-quality audio mix:
- Rural ambience: birds, insects, wind through leaves, distant village activity and livestock.
- Market ambience: vendors, footsteps, tea-stall utensils, bicycle bells and crowd murmur.
- City ambience: traffic, engines, horns, rickshaw bells and pedestrian ambience.
- Gameplay: footsteps synced to animation, jump, slide, coin pickup, collision, UI feedback and revive.
- Weather: rain, thunder, wind and water splashes.
- Food breaks: cooking, plates/cups, pouring tea/water and eating sounds.
- Music: original or properly licensed menu/gameplay music.

Use spatial audio and volume ducking where appropriate. Provide independent master, ambience, effects and music volume controls, plus mute options. Never use unlicensed commercial songs or recordings.

## Screens
Home, gameplay, pause, character selection, shop, missions, food break, game over, revive and settings.

## Game over / revive
A non-graphic fall animation, short cinematic slowdown, final distance/coins/best score, restart/home and optional revive. No blood or gore.

## Accessibility and safety
Readable UI, simple controls, adjustable audio, Bangla-first interface, optional English, no gambling, no explicit content and no graphic violence. Avoid forced ads; any ad integration must be age-appropriate and compliant with store policies.
